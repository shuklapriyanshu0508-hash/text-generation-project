The Technology Solution

Riya was working on a difficult college project when she faced a major problem. Her team needed to organize hundreds of pieces of information, but doing it manually would take several days.

Riya decided to create a simple computer program that could automatically organize the information. After several attempts, she successfully developed the program.

The solution saved her team many hours of work. More importantly, Riya learned that technology is most useful when it is applied to solve real-world problems.
