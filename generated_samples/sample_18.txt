Five Practical Job Interview Tips

1. Research the company:
Learn about the company's products, services, culture, and recent activities.

2. Practice common questions:
Prepare answers for questions about your skills, education, experience, and career goals.

3. Prepare your introduction:
Create a short and confident introduction about yourself.

4. Dress professionally:
Wear clean and appropriate clothes suitable for the interview.

5. Be confident and honest:
Listen carefully to questions, answer clearly, and be honest about your skills and experience.
