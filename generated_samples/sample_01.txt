The Hidden Book

Aarav was a curious student who often spent his free time in the
college library. One afternoon, while searching for a book for his
assignment, he noticed an old book hidden behind a row of dusty books.

Curious, Aarav opened it and discovered fascinating stories about
science, history, and human discoveries. Every chapter taught him
something new and inspired him to think differently.

Aarav soon realized that this was not just another book. It was the
most valuable book he had ever found because it encouraged him to learn
beyond his textbooks.

From that day, Aarav visited the library regularly. The mysterious book
changed his attitude toward learning and became his favorite book in the entire library.
