College Organizes Artificial Intelligence Workshop

ABC College organized a one-day workshop on Artificial Intelligence to introduce students to the fundamentals and applications of modern AI technologies.

The workshop included sessions on machine learning, generative AI, and real-world applications of artificial intelligence. Students also participated in practical activities to understand how AI systems can be used to solve problems.

Faculty members encouraged students to explore emerging technologies and develop practical skills.

The event provided students with valuable knowledge and encouraged them to learn more about the growing field of artificial intelligence.
