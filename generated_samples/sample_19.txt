Teacher and Student Dialogue

Teacher: Why do you think reading books is important?

Student: I think books help us learn new things and improve our knowledge.

Teacher: That's correct. Can reading also improve communication skills?

Student: Yes. Reading introduces us to new words and different ways of expressing ideas.

Teacher: Exactly. It can also improve imagination and critical thinking.

Student: I understand. I will try to read for at least a few minutes every day.

Teacher: That's a great habit. Consistency is more important than reading a large amount at once.
